Our Process

Starts with Clarity

We visit your space to understand your needs, preference and budget to plan your functional space.

Architectural Planning

Our architects and engineers plan your building layout, including floor plans, electrical systems, and plumbing.

Design Development

We create design sketches and 3D visuals, review them with you, and make revisions based on your feedback.

Interior Design

Define the mood and color palette, select materials and furniture, and bring the interior to life with detailed 3D visuals.

Approval, Budget & Timeline

Finalize the design, project scope, budget and timeline, and complete the required approvals before execution.

Execution & handover

Coordinate with our manufacturer and subcontractors to execute the approved design and ensure a smooth, timely handover.
